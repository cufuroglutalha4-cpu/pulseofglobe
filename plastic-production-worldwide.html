How Much Plastic Is Produced Each Year?

OECD reports global plastics production of 460 million tonnes in 2019 and plastic waste generation of 353 million tonnes. Those are different flows and should not be added together.

Production is not ocean pollution

Plastic can remain in products for years. Production in one year, waste discarded in that year and plastic entering the ocean are not interchangeable quantities. This page covers production scale; the existing ocean-plastic page covers a different indicator.

A daily scale illustration

Dividing 460 million tonnes by 365 gives about 1.26 million tonnes per day. The optional moving display uses the same historical annual baseline and the current UTC year length. It is a visualization of scale, not a new estimate of 2026 factory production.

What the recycling percentage means

The OECD’s 9% figure describes the share of plastic waste ultimately recycled after losses in the recycling process. It is not the share of all plastic ever made, and it is not a prediction for every country. A local recycling label cannot establish a global recovery rate.

Frequently asked questions

Is the display a current production feed?

No. It time-scales the historical 2019 baseline.

Is 9% the share collected for recycling?

No. It refers to waste ultimately recycled after losses, according to the cited OECD summary.
