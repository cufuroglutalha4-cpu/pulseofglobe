What the timer measures

The display starts at zero when you open the page. Pause freezes the chosen interval, Resume continues it, and Reset begins a fresh session. Time is measured locally in your browser; no personal birth date is requested or stored.

How the estimate is calculated

Each annual assumption is divided by the number of seconds in the current UTC year. The result is multiplied by the active session duration. Net change is the difference between modelled births and deaths. The year length is recalculated across a new-year boundary, including leap years.

How to interpret a short session

Small intervals produce fractional expected counts before the display rounds them down. This does not mean a fraction of a person exists: it is an average rate applied to a time interval. Real events vary and are not observed by this page. These assumptions match the existing site; their exact dataset provenance remains undocumented.

Frequently asked questions

Does pause stop real-world births?

No. It only freezes the educational session timer.

Are my details sent anywhere?

The timer has no personal-data input. The site’s existing Google Analytics tag still applies under its privacy policy.
