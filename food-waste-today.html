Food Waste Today

UNEP estimated 1.05 billion tonnes of food waste at retail and consumer levels in 2022. Dividing that annual baseline by 365 gives about 2.88 million tonnes per day; it is not a measured total for today.

What the baseline includes

The Food Waste Index covers waste in households, food service and retail. The quoted total includes inedible parts, so it must not be converted into a number of edible meals without extra assumptions. Food lost before retail is a separate accounting problem.

How the moving display works

The counter scales the 2022 annual baseline by elapsed UTC time in the current year. A full leap-year day therefore has a slightly different modelled amount from the 365-day illustration. The source year remains 2022 even though the clock advances today.

From a large number to a practical question

To use a waste statistic locally, first define what is being weighed, the time interval and the number of people or meals served. Keep a consistent definition between measurements. A worldwide average alone cannot tell a household or business its own waste rate.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a live measurement?

No. The moving number is a time-scaled illustration of an older annual estimate.

Does food waste mean only edible food?

The quoted baseline includes inedible parts; it is not a count of meals.
